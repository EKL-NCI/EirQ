Welcome to EirQ!


Here at EirQ, we aim to propose a new method for public transport systems to track and monitor the environmental quality for passengers aboard each public transport vehicle. We believe that human health should come first before anything, and we strive to implement this belief through our product here.

Using air quality sensors we have developed a software that is both advanced and user friendly for the transport systems to use to track and monitor the air quality for their passengers aboard their vehicles.

In order to use our service, you must first make an account using your name, email and company name. From there, you will recieve a verification email in your inbox to activate your account. You can then sign in as you please using the email and password you chose.

Once you are logged in, you can view your Dashboard, which is where your sensor info will be displayed. There are graphs to visualise the data flowing in from the sensor, and also a chart that updates live which you can scroll through.

This page is essentially your main hub for viewing your sensor information as all the data is laid out here.
